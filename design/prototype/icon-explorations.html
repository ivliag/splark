1-bit status icons: dimmed → brighter → bright

Pure 1-bit pixel art like your reference: every pixel is fully on or off, and brightness comes from dithering. Dimmed (just comments) is drawn in a 50% checker pattern. Brighter (question for you) has a solid outline with a dithered fill. Bright (needs action) is fully solid. Icons are 15×15 pixels at one pixel per point, so they stay crisp on Retina. The framed black tiles are enlarged; the small panels show real size. Click a set to pick it.
